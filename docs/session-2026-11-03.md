## 1. SF9 — print correction

### The fault

Only two named page rules existed, both long bond: `@page landscape` (13in × 8.5in) and `@page long` (8.5in × 13in). `FormDialogs.tsx` chose between them by grade level alone, so every card was laid out on a 13in sheet whatever paper was in the tray. Measured in Chrome:

| Card | School's paper | Page box used | Dialog shrink | Smallest text on paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grade 1 | A4 portrait | 216 × 330mm | **89.9%** | 5.8pt |
| Grades 2–3 | A4 landscape | 330 × 216mm | **89.9%** | 5.8pt |
| Grades 4–6 | A4 landscape | 330 × 216mm | **84.6%** | 5.5pt |

That single fault produced every symptom reported: text too small, proportions altered, and a third of the sheet unused.

Paper sizes were confirmed from the school's own files, not assumed — `g1_sf9.xlsx` (A4 portrait), `g2`/`g3_sf9.xlsx` (A4 landscape), `REPORT-CARD_G4,5,6.docx` (`pgSz 15840×12240 code 1`, `pgMar 720` twips = US Letter landscape, 0.5in margins, 2 columns).

### What changed

- Three page rules: `@page sf9-g1` (A4 portrait, 6mm), `@page sf9-g23` (A4 landscape, 6mm), `@page sf9-g46` (A4 landscape, 12.7mm).
- **Grades 4–6 is A4, not Letter** — decided 2026-11-02. The Word file says Letter, but the school's three other references are A4 and the staffroom dialog defaults to it; Letter caused a spill onto a third sheet.
- `PrintCopy` gained an optional `page` prop. Absent, behaviour is unchanged, so SF10, Next year, Learner record and Help are untouched.
- `sf9Page` lives in `web/forms/sf9.tsx` and is keyed off `form.layout`, so both print paths — the learner's card and the section batch in `ReportCards.tsx` — agree.
- Per-format type and spacing, stated in **points**, the same unit as the page box.

### Two bugs found and fixed during the work

- **`:last-child` matched nothing.** A printed card carries a footer `<p>`, so `.card-page:last-child` never matched and every sheet-2 rule was dead. Now `:last-of-type`. The harness had been rendering without a footer and so reported the broken rule as working — fixed there too.
- **Flex containers stopped margin collapsing**, silently adding ~37px to a half and pushing sheet 1 over. Only halves that have slack are columns now.

### Filling the page

On the school's real three-term Grade 4 card, sheet 2 used 53% and sheet 1's left half 27%. Each half is now a column and the block that can absorb the slack takes it — grade and observed-values tables spread their rows; signature lines spread down the back cover.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet 2 | 367px of 698px (53%) | 627px (90%) |
| Sheet 1, left half | 189px (27%) | 622px (89%) |

### Seals

DepEd seal left, school seal right, heading centred between them.

- `deped-seal-v2-320.png` — the published artwork sits on a **grey gradient** (corners ~156,155,155), which printed as a box. Removed by flood-filling inwards from the border, so white inside the ring and the open book survives.
- `balili-seal-v1-231.png` — keeps its alpha, so it stays PNG. A JPEG re-encode matted its transparency to black.

### Also

- The seeded-data notice is set small on SF9 page roots only, because at 10pt it pushed the Grade 1 card (94% full) onto a second sheet.
- The Cancellation of Eligibility to Transfer block now prints the school head's name. **Flagged and unresolved:** on the official SF9 that block is completed by the *receiving* school, which is why it ships blank. Pre-printing Balili's head there may be wrong.

## 2. SF10 — print and screen

- Page 1 **overflowed by 140px** onto a third sheet. Two causes: block heading tables wrapping long names, and `.sf10-grades td { height: 1.2rem }` — rem measured against the root font size, not the form's, so rows were half again too tall at 7.5pt. Both now in points. **3 sheets → 2.**
- Its own `@page sf10` (8.5 × 13in long bond, 8mm) rather than sharing `@page long` with Next year.
- **A `min-height` alone did nothing useful** — it stretched the page box while content stayed at the top. The page is a flex column, the block grid takes the slack with `grid-auto-rows: 1fr`, and each block's grade table spreads its rows. **94% fill.**
- **Data loss fixed:** `blocks.slice(0,4)` / `slice(4,8)` hard-capped the form at eight blocks, so a learner with nine or more graded years silently lost the rest off a *permanent* record. Now chunks four to a sheet over as many sheets as needed, rounded to whole leaves so the page count stays even and duplex always pairs. Verified: 9 years → 16 blocks over 4 sheets, none missing.
- DepEd seal left, DepEd wordmark right (`deped-wordmark-v1-420.png`). The toppng download **was not transparent** — its checkerboard was painted into the pixels (corner alpha 255, greys 254 and 237). Keyed out globally rather than border-filled, because the counters of D, e, o and p are enclosed.
- **Screen now matches paper.** The format rules moved out of `@media print` to `.paper.sf10`, so both render identically: 756px content width, 1120px pages, 7.0pt cells in each. Only `@page`, page breaks and the seeded notice stay print-only.

This reverses the original brief's *"keep screen styles and print styles separate"* — deliberately, at your request, because a permanent record is checked on screen and signed on paper.

## 4. Learner registry

- **"Exact LRN" filter removed** — the same search twice; "Name or LRN" already matches an exact LRN.
- **Grade level filter added.** Needed a backend change (`/learners` is a `strictObject`), but **no migration** — the learner list is plain SQL in the service. Matches anyone holding a *valid* enrolment at that grade; row security filters the subquery, so a teacher sees only their own.
- **Three clicks down to one.** Open → *School years* (a folded `<details>`) → *Load enrollment records* is now just Open. The privacy rule it protected still holds: the *list* never loads enrolments; opening one learner is a deliberate read and the audit row says so.
- **"Permanent record (SF10)" removed** from the opened panel — redundant with the list column. The per-row SF9 **stayed**: the column button opens only the current year's card, so removing it would have made past years unreachable.
- **Grade and Section columns added.** This needed a backend change after all — my own inspection report wrongly said it needed none. `learnerList` left-joins the learner's enrolment in the active year. The access row now names the enrolment too, still one row per page load.

## 5. Optional section — [0048_optional_section.sql](../database/migrations/0048_optional_section.sql)

**Not applied to live.**

A learner is enrolled in a year at a grade; which class they join is decided later and by someone else.

- `enrollments.section_id` is nullable.
- **`enrollment_events.section_id` too** — it was also `NOT NULL` and the trigger writes it on every insert, so the first unplaced learner would have failed at the journal. This was the blocker the spec didn't mention.
- The composite foreign key is **left exactly as 0004 wrote it**. It is `MATCH SIMPLE`, so Postgres skips it when `section_id` is null and enforces all three columns when it isn't. An invalid section is still rejected, with no new code.
- `place_enrollment(learner, year, grade, section, date, reason)` is the one writer. `enroll_learner` keeps its 0004 signature and delegates, so the registry form, roster import and next-year placement work unchanged.
- **Latent bug fixed:** `move_enrollment` compared sections with `=`, which against a null yields *null*, not false — the guard fell open. Now `is distinct from`.
- **LRN rule:** `place_enrollment` refuses a learner with no LRN (`P0303` → `LRN_REQUIRED`). Registration has always required one; this catches records predating that rule. Because every path comes through `place_enrollment`, the rule holds for the registry form, the roster import and promotion alike.

**Teacher visibility was not widened.** `can_read_enrollment` inner-joins `sections`, so a null section drops the row naturally — unplaced learners are invisible to teachers *because of the existing policy*.

## 6. Enrolment without a section

- `enrollmentCreate` takes *a section*, **or** *a year and a grade*.
- The service routes to `place_enrollment` when there's no section and to the original `enroll_learner` when there is.
- **Register a learner** gained an optional **Enrol at grade**. Choosing one registers *and* enrols for the active year at that grade with no section. The profile is created first and the enrolment is a second request, so a refusal cannot lose a learner just typed in.
- **A bug I introduced two steps earlier:** the placement query used an *inner* join to `sections`, so an unplaced-but-enrolled learner would have read "Not enrolled this year". Now a left join.

## 7. Promotion without sections — [0049_promotion_without_sections.sql](../database/migrations/0049_promotion_without_sections.sql)

**Not applied to live.**

- `commit_next_year_placement` no longer reads `section_id`. It works out the target grade from the approved outcome and enrols with no section.
- `read_next_year_source` joins the source section **outwards** — a learner unplaced this year still has an outcome to carry forward.
- **The draw is gone.** `placement.ts` lost the seed, shuffle, sex-and-size balancing and section choice.
- **A grade with no sections no longer blocks anyone.** Under the draw, a learner whose next grade had no section was left behind, so the school had to build next year's classes before it could promote. Verified with zero sections in the target year.
- The screen loses the draw number, "Draw again", the section filter, the sections table and per-section printables; it gains a **Grades next year** summary, an **Assigned later** badge per row, and printing grouped by grade.
- `next_year_placements.seed` is `NOT NULL` and old runs carry theirs, so the API still accepts and records a seed — it just decides nothing. **Vestigial; consider removing.**
